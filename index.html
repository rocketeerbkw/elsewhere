A ROAD TRIP WITH SOME PERSPECTIVE

Your miles travel well.

Enter your start, finish, and any stops along the way. We find a driving route, then let you pick up the whole thing and lay it over another part of the world.

Drag the orange line to move it. Use the rotation slider to turn it. The route rotates as one piece on a spherical Earth, preserving its distances and bends. Its appearance changes with the map projection, just like The True Size.

The orange line is a comparison overlay. It won't follow the roads in its new location. Add stops to make the original route match your trip more closely.

Save a trip file to keep the exact route. Share links contain your stops and placement, and calculate the route again when opened.
